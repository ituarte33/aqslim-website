¿Qué es la Auriculoterapia?

What is Auriculotherapy?

La Auriculoterapia es una técnica milenaria originaria de la medicina tradicional oriental. Al estimular puntos específicos en el oído externo, activamos el sistema nervioso para regular el apetito, el metabolismo y el equilibrio emocional — sin agujas, sin dolor.

Auriculotherapy is an ancient technique rooted in traditional Eastern medicine. By stimulating specific points on the outer ear, we activate the nervous system to regulate appetite, metabolism, and emotional balance — no needles, no pain.

We select from over 60 auriculotherapy protocols to design your unique session, combining points for weight control, anxiety, digestion, and more.
03
Seguimiento y Evolución
Ongoing Follow-Up

Each week we adjust your protocol based on your progress. We alternate left and right ear to optimize results in weight and measurements.
